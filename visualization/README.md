# A tail cutoff multiplies two choices

Extract the ZIP and open index.html. The explorer works offline. Adjust x, y and z, compare both exact CVaR values, and rotate the selected lower-tail payoff bars.

The example uses three variables, h = 1/48 and a uniform reward shift of +2. The lowest half spends its last 4h on three compensation blocks, the low branch and a fraction of the pivot. The pivot contributes a product because both its selected mass and reward are variable.
