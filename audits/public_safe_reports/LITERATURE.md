# Q668 source and priority assessment

**Verdict, 27 November 2026.** The checked primary sources establish the same feasibility problem and prior membership machinery. They disclose NP-hardness even for pure CVaR, but no matching existential-real hardness for this simultaneous exact mean-payoff problem. The binary-reward result at a common interior tail level strengthens the prior pure-CVaR case. Confidence in the bounded search is moderate.

## Exact model and prior bounds

Jan Křetínský and Tobias Meggendorfer, *Conditional Value-at-Risk for Reachability and Mean Payoff in Markov Decision Processes*, LICS 2018, 609–618, DOI 10.1145/3209108.3209176. Definition 3.2 averages the worst probability mass, including the required fraction of an atom at the quantile. Sections 4 and 6 use rational finite MDPs with state rewards, arbitrary randomized strategies and coordinatewise liminf mean payoffs. Theorem 6.2 gives NP-hardness for pure CVaR when dimension is part of the input, by reduction from 3-SAT. It first proves hardness for weighted reachability and then transfers to mean payoff by absorbing rewards. Its construction uses an expectation represented by level one. Theorem 6.4 gives an NP upper bound for multidimensional weighted reachability and polynomial time for fixed dimension in that model. Those bounds cannot simply be transferred to mean payoff, where controlled limiting frequencies vary over a continuum. [LICS record](https://lics.siglog.org/archive/2018/KretnskMeggendorfer-ConditionalValueatR.html) · [Author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/1805.02946v1#S6).

Lemma 6.7 gives sufficiency of maximal-end-component-constant payoffs for expectation and CVaR. Theorem 6.8 and Figure 9 give the transient and recurrent linear flows and use a polynomial-size real-algebraic program with quantified VaR cutoffs to establish PSPACE containment. They describe guessing cutoff order information, which can itself be encoded existentially. The present hinge formulation gives a direct quadratic description without the sorting guesses. The essential membership method is prior work. Theorem 6.9 treats additional VaR constraints, which are outside the present result. The source does not establish existential-real hardness. [Author manuscript §6.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1805.02946v1#S6.SS3).

Tobias Meggendorfer's 2021 TUM dissertation, *Verification of Discrete-Time Markov Decision Processes*, §6.2, printed pp. 49–51, states the simultaneous thresholds and reiterates the gap. It conjectures NP-completeness for the mean-payoff case. Appendix F reproduces the LICS paper. The present classification implies that NP membership is equivalent to NP = ∃ℝ. It gives no separation of the two classes. [Dissertation](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1550256/1550256.pdf) · [Pinned catalog Q668](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/mathematics/optimization-operations-research.md#q668).

## Hardness sources and nearby work

- The reduction uses bounded ETR-INV from Mikkel Abrahamsen, Anna Adamaszek and Tillmann Miltzow, *The Art Gallery Problem is ∃ℝ-complete*, J. ACM 69(1), Article 4 (2022), §4, Definition 14 and Theorem 16. The preprint numbers are Definition 5 and Theorem 7. Variables are in [1/2,2] and equations are constant-one, addition or reciprocal equations. The CVaR construction produces the multiplication of two bounded choices from the payoff of a pivot branch and the amount of its mass in the lower tail. [Journal text](https://research-portal.uu.nl/ws/files/145819294/3486220.pdf) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3486220).
- Thomas Brihaye, Krishnendu Chatterjee, Stefanie Mohr and Maximilian Weininger, *Risk-aware Markov Decision Processes Using Cumulative Prospect Theory*, arXiv:2505.09514, use vector reachability as a technical tool for optimizing a scalar CPT objective. Their Theorem IV.10 concerns value approximation and Theorem V.1 collapses scalar mean payoff to weighted reachability via a best value per maximal end component. A single scalar optimum in a component need not maximize multiple payoff dimensions at once. This result does not give the exact simultaneous CVaR classification. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09514).
- Xia, Yu and Wu's *On the Maximization of Long-Run Reward CVaR for Markov Decision Processes* and the 2026 *Long-Run Conditional Value at Risk Reinforcement Learning* study CVaR of instantaneous rewards or costs under a stationary law. These risk functionals differ from applying CVaR to the cross-run distribution of the pathwise liminf mean payoff. Meggendorfer's *Risk-Aware Stochastic Shortest Path* treats total accumulated cost before termination. None supplies the same vector feasibility result. [Stationary reward CVaR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01586) · [Stationary cost learning](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.09734v2) · [Stochastic shortest path](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01640).
- The existential-real compendium lists several related MDP complexity results, including parameter synthesis for polynomial transition functions and memoryless minimization of trace distance. The primary results use either symbolic transition probabilities or an unrelated trace-distance objective. This problem has fixed rational transitions and the policy is the quantified object. [Compendium §19](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.18006) · [Parametric reachability](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CONCUR.2019.14).
