# Claim scope

The input has finite states, rational transitions and rational vector state rewards. Payoff is the coordinatewise liminf of pathwise time averages. Lower-tail CVaR averages exactly the stated probability mass, including the relevant fraction of a quantile atom. Strategies can randomize with real probabilities and depend on history. The number of reward dimensions can grow with the instance.

The binary-reward restriction uses only CVaR constraints at a common rational level strictly between zero and one. The result gives polynomial-time many-one completeness for the existential theory of the reals. Fixed reward dimension, deterministic strategies, VaR, discounted objectives, finite-horizon terminal rewards and endpoints of the tail-level interval are outside the claim.
